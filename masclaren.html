Information Maclaren Team 2026

Scuderia Ferrari, officially known as Scuderia Ferrari HP, is one of the most iconic and historic names in Formula 1. Based in Maranello, Italy, they are the only team to have competed in every F1 season since the World Championship began in 1950.
2026 Team Overview
For the 2026 season, the team is competing with the SF-26 chassis, which marks the start of a new regulatory cycle in the sport.

  • Team Principal: Frédéric Vasseur
  • Deputy Team Principal: Jérôme Ambrosio
  • Technical Directors: Loïc Serra (Chassis) and Enrico Gualtieri (Power Unit)
  • Race Drivers:
    • Lando Norris
    • Oscar Piastri
    • Reserve Driver: Antonio Giovinazzi
