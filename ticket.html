Ticket General Admission (GA)

  • No Reserved Seating: You do not get a physical seat or a numbered chair. Instead, GA gives you entry to designated open-air zones around the circuit.
  • Roaming Freedom: Unlike grandstand ticket holders who are stuck in one seat all day, GA allows you to walk around, explore different parts of the track, and watch the cars from multiple angles throughout the weekend.
  • The Viewing Experience: You will typically watch the race from natural elevations, grassy hillsides, elevated earth mounds, or standing-room-only zones near the safety fences.
  • Fan Zone Access: GA tickets include standard entry to the main F1 Fan Village/Zone, where you can buy merchandise, enjoy food and drinks, check out entertainment displays, and attend post-race concerts (at tracks that offer them).

Grandstand Tickets (Reserved Seating)

  • Guaranteed Reserved Seat: You are assigned a specific block, row, and seat number. You can leave to get food or use the restroom and return knowing your spot is waiting for you.
  • Strategic Viewing Locations: Grandstands are positioned at the most exciting parts of the track, such as long straightaways, heavy braking zones, hairpin corners, and pit lane exits.
  • Giant Screen Access: Almost all grandstands face a giant television screen, allowing you to watch replays, follow the live leaderboard, and see what is happening on other parts of the track.
  • Covered Options: Many grandstands (though not all) feature a roof or overhang, protecting you from direct sun and sudden rain showers.

VIP & Hospitality Packages

  • All-Inclusive Luxury & Gourmet Dining: Access to climate-controlled lounges or suites featuring world-class catering, complimentary open bars (fine wines, champagne, and spirits), and continuous high-end food service throughout the day.
  • Prime Viewing Locations: VIP suites are strategically located directly above the pit lane (overlooking the team garages) or at the circuit's most famous corners, often featuring both indoor air-conditioned spaces and private outdoor viewing balconies.
  • Exclusive Behind-the-Scenes Access: You will typically watch the race from natural elevations, grassy hillsides, elevated earth mounds, or standing-room-only zones near the safety fences.
  • Fan Zone Access: GA tickets include standard entry to the main F1 Fan Village/Zone, where you can buy merchandise, enjoy food and drinks, check out entertainment displays, and attend post-race concerts (at tracks that offer them).
