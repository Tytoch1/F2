Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team, based in Brackley, UK, represents the silver arrows in Formula 1 with a track record of dominant championship runs.
2026 Team Overview
For the 2026 season, the team is competing with their latest chassis innovations entering the new power unit regulations.

  • Race Drivers:
